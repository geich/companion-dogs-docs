Companion Dogs: Breed Manual

A guide to every breed: what each one does best, the moodles, the traits, and how to pick the right one for your game.

To learn how the mod works (taming, commands, food, saddlebags, tricks, hunting), see the other manual: Features.

The numbers in this manual assume the default options. If the server turns off Skills & training, breeds stop improving: skills stay locked at zero, the damage-per-level curve becomes generic, and all four tricks unlock at once (the mod starts treating every dog as Obedience 10). What a breed gives a dog at birth still counts: base bite power, knockdown, nerve in a fight, exclusive moodle, saddlebag and litter size. Hiding the tricks is a separate option, Obedience tricks.

1. What a breed actually changes

A breed touches seven things, and nothing beyond that:

What changes What it means
Learning speed Each breed learns each of the 5 skills at a different rate. This is what defines a breed the most
Bite power How much damage per bite, and how much of that is earned through training
Nerve in a fight How much stress it piles up while fighting, and the point where it panics and stops
The traits (genes) The range of Strength, Aggressiveness and Resistance it can be born with
Exclusive moodle A bonus for you, while the dog is close, fed and loyal
Litter size How many puppies per birth
Where it shows up on the map Each breed has its own places

And what does NOT change with the breed, whatever a lot of people assume:

  • Running speed. There is no faster breed. They all run the same.
  • Sentinel, scent and hunting range. These depend only on the skill level. The breed changes how fast you get there, not the ceiling.
  • The tricks. All four unlock by Obedience level, the same for every dog.
  • Hunger and thirst. The rate is the same for every breed (even the Husky, see its entry).
  • Saddlebag capacity, except for the Husky, the only one with a breed bonus.
  • Body size is not Strength. They are two separate things, and they are easy to mix up because they travel together. See below.
There is one exception, and it is not a dog. The cat changes an eighth thing that no dog breed touches: which jobs exist at all. It does not fight, does not mind livestock and does not breed — the mechanics don't get worse for it, they disappear. As long as you are comparing dogs, the list of seven above holds in full.

Size and Strength are not the same thing

Strength is a gene and it makes a real difference: it raises bite damage by up to 30% and it is what sets saddlebag capacity.

Body size is something else. It comes from a gene of the game's own, which the mod neither rolls nor touches, plus the puppy's natural growth up to the breed's adult size. Two dogs of the same breed can end up different sizes with no difference in Strength at all, and a dog that is bigger than another one of the same breed does not bite harder for it.

What confuses people is that the breed sets both things at once, each on its own track: a German Shepherd is big and born strong, a Caramelo is small and born weak. So, comparing breed with breed, the bigger dog usually does hit harder. Only the cause is the breed, not the size: looking at how big a dog is tells you nothing about that individual dog's Strength. That is what the Strength bar on the Genetics tab of the dog window is for.

One breed, one look

There is no colour variation inside a breed. Each breed has exactly one texture, and male and female use the same one. A Husky always looks like the same Husky.

The five cats are no exception, however much they look like one: black, white, orange, calico and tuxedo are five breeds, each with its own single texture. It is not one cat that comes in five colours.

A puppy of any breed is born at the same reduced size, and grows into the adult size of its breed.

3. The five breeds in the base mod

These come with the main mod, nothing else to install.

Caramelo

The Brazilian street mutt. This is the first dog you will run into, and probably your first companion.

"The caramelo is a basic, everyday breed, the mongrel you find all over the cities. It has no special skills and is weak by nature: it can't kill a zombie on its own, only knock one down to buy you time."

Where to find it: residential houses, in towns and neighbourhoods. 10% per house, the most common breed in the game.

Learns: everything at normal speed. Nothing it stands out at, and nothing it is bad at.

In a fight: it is the only dog that cannot finish a zombie off. Its bite pins the zombie just short of dead and stops there. That is deliberate and no sandbox setting changes it. What it does do is knock them down, and a zombie on the ground is exactly what buys you the time to run or swing.

It is also the most nervous dog in the mod: it builds combat stress five times faster than a German Shepherd, and it is the first one to panic.

Traits: the weakest of all. In the dog window it will show Low in Strength, Aggressiveness and Resistance, always, on every Caramelo you tame. There is no point hunting for a good Caramelo.

Litter: 2 to 4 puppies, the biggest in the mod. If your goal is to build up a kennel, this is the breed that produces the most.

Saddlebag: 4 to 5 cargo. The smallest.

Moodle: Good Company

"The Caramelo is a wonderfully affectionate, charismatic mutt. Its loyal company lifts your spirits, so sadness and boredom fade faster while it's near."

It reduces Unhappiness and Boredom continuously while it stays close. It is one of the most useful moodles in the mod day to day, because boredom and sadness are constant problems in Zomboid and expensive to fix any other way.

Who it is for: everyone, early on. And it stays good later, because its moodle solves a problem no other breed solves. Just don't count on it to kill anything.

German Shepherd

The war dog. The combat breed of the base mod.

"A powerful, intelligent working dog. Born far stronger and tougher than the caramelo, it learns Combat and Obedience quickly, barely frays in a fight, holds the line against a swarm without panicking, and can bring a zombie down on its own."

Where to find it: police stations, prisons, jails, military bases, barracks, gun stores and ammo rooms. 20% per building. It never shows up in houses. You have to go looking.

Learns: Combat at double speed, and Obedience 50% faster. Hunting a bit faster too. It is the breed that gets strong the quickest.

In a fight: the hardest bite in the base mod, and it kills a zombie on its own. But what sets it apart is not the damage, it is the nerve. It builds stress five times more slowly than the Caramelo, and its panic threshold is the highest in the mod. In practice: it keeps fighting in situations where any other breed would already have frozen up.

Traits: mid range across the board, well above the Caramelo. A German Shepherd will show Average in all three traits.

Litter: 1 to 2.

Saddlebag: 6 to 8.

Moodle: Courage

A bold German Shepherd at your side steadies your nerves: its fearless presence eases your panic while it stays close.

It drives Panic down continuously. It is the strongest moodle by the numbers in the base mod, and it solves a serious problem: panic wrecks your aim and your melee efficiency. With a Shepherd close by, you fight with a steadier hand.

Who it is for: people who fight. If you play up close and in the middle of town, this is the breed.

Note: the in-game description says a third of the stress, but the real number is a fifth. The text is too conservative, the dog is even better than it promises.

Golden Retriever

The gun dog with the fine nose. The breed that improves your foraging.

"A friendly, keen-nosed gun dog and a natural hunter: it learns Hunting twice as fast and can bring a zombie down on its own (though it frays more easily than the German Shepherd). Its nose helps you forage and track game from farther away."

Where to find it: residential houses, on a roll separate from the Caramelo. 2.5% per house. Four times rarer than the mutt, but in the same houses: one house can have both.

Learns: Hunting at double speed, and Scent 50% faster. It is the breed that reaches Hunting level 6 earliest, which is when a dog starts taking on deer.

In a fight: average damage, kills on its own. It frays more than the Shepherd, less than the Caramelo.

Traits: a bit above the Caramelo, below the Shepherd. This is not a fighting dog.

Litter: 1 to 2.

Saddlebag: 6 to 7.

Moodle: Keen Nose

"Your Golden Retriever works the ground with you. While it stays close, fed and loyal, you spot forage from farther away and learn Foraging, Trapping and Aiming faster."

Three effects:

  • +20% XP in Foraging, Trapping and Aiming.
  • +4 tiles on your Search Mode vision radius, on top of the bonus any dog already gives.

This is the moodle that changes the pace of a countryside character the most. Foraging and Trapping are slow skills to level, and Aiming is expensive.

Who it is for: anyone living off hunting, foraging and trapping. It fits a rural base.

Note: the in-game description says it learns Combat a little faster than the caramelo. That is not true: it learns Combat at the same speed as the mutt.

Husky

The pack mule. The strongest dog in the mod, and the only one that keeps you warm.

"A hardy northern sled dog: by far the strongest breed, built to haul cargo (its saddlebag carries the most) and to survive the cold. Stubborn (slower to learn Obedience), friendly (a poor guard) and a big eater."

Where to find it: two sources.

  • Residential houses, in winter only. 1.25% per house. The rarest breed in the game out of season.
  • Pet shops and vet clinics. 10% per building, all year round. This is the reliable way to get one.

Learns: Scent at double speed (the best sentinel in the mod), Hunting 50% faster, and Combat a little above average. But it learns Obedience 20% more slowly, and that is the only breed penalty in the whole mod. It is deliberate: the dog is stubborn.

The practical consequence of slow Obedience: it takes longer to unlock the tricks, it stays disobedient for longer, and its disobey threshold comes down more slowly. A Husky asks for more patience.

In a fight: above average damage, kills on its own, but it is no warrior. It frays a lot more than a Shepherd.

Traits: the widest Strength range in the mod, by a mile. A Husky will show Average or High in Strength, where the other breeds sit at Average or Low.

Litter: 1 to 2.

Saddlebag: 13 to 16 cargo. It is the only breed with a saddlebag bonus (+50%), and the gap is huge: it carries two to three times more than any other dog.

Moodle: Warmed

"Your husky pulls its weight at your side. While it stays close, fed and loyal, keeping up with it builds your Strength faster and it helps you keep your wind, so you tire more slowly; and when the cold sets in, its thick coat keeps you warm."

Three effects:

  • Strength rises 50% faster while it is nearby. Strength is the slowest skill in the game, and it is the one that decides how much you carry and how hard you hit in melee.
  • Endurance recovers continuously, you tire more slowly.
  • A walking heat source that switches on by itself once you start getting cold and off when you warm up. It only kicks in during real cold (below -3 °C outdoors) and is worth more the closer you stand to the dog. It softens the drop in your temperature, it is not a coat: winter clothing is still mandatory.

The Strength gain rides on what you would earn anyway: the husky speeds you up, it does not hand anything over. It pays while you move, travel loaded and fight; a dog lying next to you while you stand still trains nothing. In exchange, what it speeds up is permanent: the Strength stays with you long after the dog is gone, and every level of it raises your carry capacity for good.

Who it is for: anyone who does long loot runs and comes back loaded, and anyone playing a heavy winter. This is the logistics dog.

Note: the description says a big eater (gets hungry fast). That does not apply to a dog you have already tamed. The hunger rate of companions is the same for every breed. The bigger appetite only exists while it is still a wild dog.

Border Collie

The farm dog. The herding breed.

A bright, tireless herding dog and the classic farm companion, prized above all for its intelligence.

Where to find it: barns, stables, silos, chicken coops and pens, out in the countryside. 15% per building, and that roll ignores the urban filter, so a farm lost in the middle of nowhere counts.

Learns: Herding at double speed, Obedience 50% faster, and Scent a little above average.

In a fight: average damage, same as the Golden. Kills on its own. It frays less than the Caramelo, but a lot more than a Shepherd. It is not a fighting dog, it is a working dog.

Traits: mid ranges, a little above the Golden.

Litter: 1 to 3, the second biggest in the base mod.

Saddlebag: 6 to 8.

The real specialty: it is 50% more efficient in the calming aura per level than any other breed. That counts in both places where the aura exists:

  • in the Care for livestock here mode, where it stands watch in the pen;
  • in its moodle, which calms the livestock around you.

Since a stressed animal gives no milk or wool, miscarries and scatters, a trained Border Collie is literally the difference between a farm that produces and one that does not.

Moodle: Herding Instinct

"Your border collie works the herd with you. While it stays close, fed and loyal, the animals around you settle down, and you learn Animal Care and Farming faster."

Two effects:

  • +20% XP in Animal Care and Farming.
  • Calms the livestock in a radius around you, automatically, while you work.

Who it is for: anyone building a farm. If you keep cows, sheep or chickens, this is the breed that protects your output.

Note: the in-game description still says it is a balanced all-rounder with no standout specialty. That is out of date: the doubled Herding and the stronger aura are already in.

4. The four addon breeds

These come as separate mods on Steam that depend on the base Companion Dogs. The link for each one is in its entry below, and all of them together in Installing the addons.

Rottweiler

(mod CD: Rottweiler, separate addon)

The warrior that saves your endurance.

"A powerful guard dog bred for protection work, a true warrior on par with the German Shepherd: it learns Combat twice as fast, hits hard at high skill, knocks zombies down and keeps fighting where most breeds panic."

Where to find it:

  • Industrial building (warehouse, shed, factory, junkyard, workshop, loading dock): 10% per building.
  • Residential house: 2.5% per house, a rare chance.

Learns: Combat at double speed, and Scent 50% faster (the only fighting dog with a good nose). It gets no Obedience bonus.

In a fight: it ties with the German Shepherd on bite, and ties on nerve: it builds stress five times slower than the mutt and its panic point sits at the maximum. It knocks down and it kills.

Traits: the same as the German Shepherd's.

Litter: 1 to 2. Saddlebag: 6 to 8.

Moodle: Front Line

"Your Rottweiler is fighting at your side. While it is engaged in combat close to you, fed and loyal, your melee swings drain less endurance."

Gives back 35% of the endurance spent on each melee swing.

This is the most conditional moodle in the mod: the icon shows up whenever the Rottweiler is nearby, but the effect only counts while it is actually fighting. And it works for melee only, firearms get nothing.

In practice it is huge. Endurance is what limits how many zombies you can swing at in a row. With a Rottweiler fighting at your side, you swing almost a third more before you run out of breath.

Who it is for: anyone who clears hordes in melee. It is the breed that fits an aggressive playstyle best.

Doberman

(mod CD: Doberman, separate addon)

The shooter's dog.

"A lean, watchful guard dog and the quickest study of any breed. In a fight it is all edge and no armour: weak while untrained, but at full Combat skill it bites hard, though it soaks up less punishment than a Rottweiler."

Where to find it:

  • Police station: 18% per building.
  • Military building (ammo store, barracks, bunker, guard post, checkpoint): 22% per building.
Practical warning: military buildings are extremely rare on the game's base map (there are only about five rooms like that in the whole of Kentucky). In practice, look in police stations.

Learns: Combat at double speed and Obedience 50% faster, exactly the same package as the German Shepherd. What separates the two is not training speed, it is the damage curve.

In a fight: it has the steepest curve in the mod. Untrained it hits a lot softer than a Rottweiler, but at Combat 10 it reaches the top. It is an investment: the Doberman you have just tamed is weak, the trained Doberman is front rank.

Strong nerve (panic point at the maximum), but it soaks up less punishment: its Resistance is the lowest among the fighting dogs.

Traits: the highest Aggressiveness range in the mod, which means it earns combat XP faster still. Strength and Resistance a little below the Rottweiler.

Litter: 1 to 2. Saddlebag: 6 to 8.

Moodle: Steady Aim

"Your Doberman is watching your back. While it is nearby, fed and loyal, your stress drains away, your aim recovers faster after each shot and you reload more quickly."

Three effects, all for firearms:

  • Your stress drains continuously (high stress makes you aim worse in Zomboid).
  • Recoil recovers 45% faster after each shot. You are back on target sooner.
  • Reloading is 15% faster.

Who it is for: anyone who plays with a gun. The three effects stack onto the same problem, which is how much time do I lose between one shot and the next.

Note: the in-game description says it trains Obedience faster than even the German Shepherd and that at full Combat it bites harder than any other dog in the pack. Both are a tie, not an advantage: it matches the German Shepherd on Obedience and matches the Rottweiler on level 10 damage.

Labrador

(mod CD: Labrador, separate addon)

The retriever. The best hunting dog and, by design, the worst fighter in the mod.

"A hunting dog built to bring it back: it trains Hunting twice as fast, with Obedience close behind. By design it is the worst fighter in the family: the weakest bite, no knockdown, and a brawl leaves it badly rattled. Its gift is its mouth."

Where to find it:

  • Residential house: 5% per house. It is the easiest addon to find, double the Golden's chance.
  • Out in the wild: hunting cabin, campsite, boathouse, fishing spot, ranger station. 20% per building.

Learns: Hunting at double speed and Obedience 50% faster. It is the hunter package with discipline.

In a fight: the worst in the mod, on purpose.

  • The weakest bite of them all.
  • It does not knock zombies down. It is the only breed without that.
  • It builds stress three and a half times faster than a Rottweiler, and panics at the same low point as the mutt.

It does kill a zombie if you insist, but do not send this dog into a fight.

Traits: the lowest Aggressiveness range in the mod. It is the gentlest dog here, and that is why it takes longer to learn to fight.

Litter: 1 to 3, the only addon that goes above 2. Saddlebag: 6 to 8.

Moodle: Soft Mouth

"Your Labrador brings back every kill from its first hunt on, and often something extra. While it is nearby, fed and loyal, you also learn Butchering and Short Blade faster."

Four effects, and this is the most complete moodle in the mod:

  • It brings the kill back from Hunting 3 on, which means as soon as it can hunt at all. Every other breed only starts bringing them back at Hunting 6. That is half a playthrough of difference.
  • It keeps at the delivery three times longer before dropping the carcass on the way.
  • 60% of deliveries come with an extra item dropped on the ground beside you: a small animal bone, a chicken feather, twigs or a wild egg. Your Labrador brought you something else: ...
  • +20% XP in Butchering and Short Blade, exactly the skills for processing what it brings you.

Who it is for: a life built around hunting. The Golden learns to hunt just as fast, but the Labrador delivers from day one, and the difference shows in the first weeks of a run.

Pug

(mod CD: Pug, separate addon)

The house dog. Every other breed earns its keep by fighting, hunting, herding or hauling. The Pug does none of that, and it is the best company in the mod.

"The house dog: the first companion whose worth is not fighting, hunting, herding or hauling. It trains Obedience twice as fast as any other breed, and its bond never fades on its own."

Where to find it:

  • Residential house: 5% per house, as easy as the Labrador. It is the house dog, after all.
  • Pet shop and vet: 12% per building, the same spot as the Husky.

It learns: Obedience at double speed, faster than any other breed in the mod, with Scent 50% faster and Hunting at 20%. It climbs the trick tree (go to a spot, distract zombies, sniff out an item, fetch it while you sleep) ahead of anything else you can own.

Its bond does not fade. Every other dog loses loyalty on its own when you are away or careless. The Pug does not. Come back from a month on the road and it is exactly as you left it. It is the only breed like that.

It warns you first. At the same level of Scent, the Pug senses zombies 35% further away than any other breed. It was bred as an alarm dog and it still works.

In a fight: do not count on it. It is the weakest dog in the mod, below the Caramelo.

  • The weakest bite of all, by a wide margin.
  • It does not kill zombies and does not knock them down. It is the only dog with both of those turned off.
  • It gains stress faster than any other and panics before all of them.

And the price does not stop there:

  • Its bark gives you away, and there is no hushing it. The same alarm that warns you warns the dead, louder than any other breed, and the Pug is the only dog that has no Quiet and no Silent watch: both buttons are greyed out on its window. You do not get to keep only the good half of its nose. (If dog noise attracting zombies is switched off in the sandbox options, that applies to the Pug like it does to everyone: the bark stays loud, but it pulls nobody.)
  • It cannot keep up at a run. In a sprint the Pug falls behind any other dog.
  • Summer hurts. A flat face does not shed heat. Above 28 °C it starts to stress and stops comforting you; at 36 °C the penalty maxes out. Indoors is cooler, and it works again.
  • Saddlebag of 2 to 3. It will wear one, but it is no pack animal.

In exchange, it eats and drinks little: half the hunger and a third less thirst than a medium-sized dog.

Traits: the smallest Strength and Resistance ranges in the mod. It is born weak and frail, always, and that is not bad luck on the puppy.

Litter: 1 to 2.

Moodle: Your Shadow

"Your Pug never leaves your side. While it is nearby, fed and loyal, unhappiness, boredom and stress fade faster than with any other dog. Heat undoes it: a panting Pug comforts nobody until it finds somewhere cool."
  • Unhappiness and Boredom drop 60% faster than next to a Caramelo.
  • Stress drops too. No other dog in the mod touches stress by simple presence; the only other way to lower stress with a dog is to sleep beside it (Well Rested).
  • The moodle disappears in the heat. Above 28 °C it leaves the HUD until the dog finds somewhere cool. That is deliberate: you notice the breed problem without reading anything.

Who it is for: for base play, for a long run, and for anyone tired of losing loyalty by staying away too long. Take a guard dog along if you are leaving town.

5. The cat: the first other species

(mod CD: Cats, separate addon)

Every breed up to here is a dog, and the difference between them is how much of each thing they do. The cat is the first real exception: what defines it is not having worse numbers, it is not having some of the jobs.

"Not a dog with worse numbers: an animal that simply does not have some of the jobs. A cat never fights the dead, is never posted to a pen, and does not breed. It hunts mice and rats, and leaves rabbits, raccoons and deer alone. What it gives you instead is warning: it notices the dead further out than any other companion, and it is quiet about everything else."

What it does NOT do

This is not weakness, it is absence. The mechanics do not exist for it, and the interface goes with them: you will not find the button greyed out, you will not find the button.

What What disappears
Does not fight No Combat in the window, no Guard mode, no auto-protection. It will not attack a zombie even while you are being mauled in front of it
Does not mind livestock No Herding, and the Tend livestock mode never appears in the menu
Does not breed No heat, no pregnancy, no kittens. A cat will not breed with a dog or with another cat
Hunts nothing bigger than a rodent Rabbits, raccoons and deer are invisible to it: it will not even point, let alone give chase

Its prey is mice, rats and squirrels. Short on variety and long on competence: since no dog in the mod is limited to rodents, the cat is the best rat hunter there is without taking anything away from the Labrador or the Golden.

What it does better than any dog

It warns you before anyone else. With no fighting left over, warning is the job, and the cat has the longest sentinel range in the mod:

Scent 0 Scent 10
Ordinary dog 18 tiles 27 tiles
Pug 24 tiles 36 tiles
Cat 25 tiles 38 tiles

And it is the quietest. The noise its meow spreads is half a dog's and a third of the Pug's bark. The Pug warns you early and gives away your position doing it; the cat warns you earlier still and stays quiet. It is the only companion in the mod that does both.

It learns Hunting at double speed, tied with the Golden and the Labrador, and Scent 50% faster.

The price

It is the most stubborn animal in the mod. Obedience at 0.7 — the steepest learning penalty there is, worse than the Husky's 0.8, which until now was the only one. It does unlock all four tricks, but it gets there well after any dog.

And the most independent. Its bond fades 50% faster than a dog's when you disappear or slack off on care. It is the exact opposite of the Pug, whose bond never fades at all. A cat demands presence.

Saddlebag of 2, full stop. It is not a range like the other breeds: the maths works out to 2 for any cat, however strong it is born. Small animal.

Nervous. It has the second worst hidden stress gene in the mod, behind only the Caramelo. It does not fight, so this never shows up in combat — it shows up as fright: loud noise, shouting, injury. A panicking cat stops keeping watch and stops bringing the gift.

It does not lie down. On Stay, the cat stays on its feet. Cosmetic only, it changes nothing.

Traits: tied with the Pug for the smallest Strength range in the mod, with Aggressiveness and Resistance just as low. There is no such thing as a strong cat, and that is not puppy bad luck.

The five coats

Black, white, orange, calico and tuxedo. To the mod they are five breeds — their own name, their own entry in the spawn menu and in the kennel — and for every practical purpose they are the same animal: same skills, same moodle, same voice, same trait ranges. The coat is appearance only.

Which coat you find is luck. Each one rolls for its own building, independently. They already come in with the chance divided by five, so the total amount of cat in the world is the same as when only the black one existed: five coats do not give you five times the cats.

Where to find them: 1% per residential house and 2.4% per pet shop or vet clinic, per coat. A cat is an indoors animal: when it turns up in a house, it is inside it, not in the yard.

The night gift

The most hidden mechanic the cat has. You wake up, and there is a dead animal at your feet.

The cat goes out while you sleep and leaves the prey. There is no chase and nothing dies in the world — which is why the gift keeps working even with Hunting switched off in the sandbox options.

How it fires: on waking up from sleep, with the cat 8 tiles or closer. Same radius as Well Rested, and for the same reason: whoever went out at night was the cat that was with you.

The chance is its Hunting, and it closes into certainty at the top:

Hunting Chance of a gift
0 35%
3 54.5%
5 67.5%
8 87%
10 100%

At level 10 there is no roll: it brings one every day.

Hunting also decides WHAT arrives. It is not just frequency — the whole list inverts:

Hunting What usually turns up
Low Worm, cricket, grasshopper, cockroach. Whatever any animal finds standing still
High Mice and rats become the common case, with frogs and centipedes in between

The difference is worth having: a worm is 3 calories, a rat is 324 and takes 22 off your hunger on top. And a frog doubles as fishing bait.

Once per day, per owner. With two cats nearby you get one gift, not two.

It gains Hunting XP every time the delivery succeeds — and that is how a well-kept cat trains itself up to the level where it never fails again.

When nothing turns up: a cat with hunger or thirst at 75% or more, sick, disobedient or panicking does not go out. It fails silently, with no message on screen. If the gifts stopped, check its bars.

This is not the fetch while you sleep trick (Obedience 10), which brings an item off the ground. Both fire on waking and they do not compete: a cat with Obedience 10 does both on the same morning.

Moodle: Cat's Eyes

"Your cat sees what you cannot. While it is fed, watered and bonded to you, the dark stops hiding what you are searching for: foraging at night works close to how it does by day."

Foraging in the dark stops being pointless. In pitch dark the game drops your foraging range to 1.5 tiles — in practice you have to be standing on top of the icon to see it. With the cat nearby, the small hours come up to roughly 60% of the range you would have by day.

Note that this is a step, not a gradual increase: without a cat you are on the wrong side of the cut-off and barely forage at night; with one you cross over to the other side. It is the difference between working and not working.

Who it is for: for anyone who plays the small hours, for anyone who wants the earliest possible warning without announcing their own position, and for anyone with rats in the house. It is not for anyone who wants a guard: take a dog along if you are leaving town.

8. Traits (the genes)

Beyond the breed, every dog has three traits shown in its window, in the Traits section. They are rolled the moment you tame the dog, within the breed's range, and they never change.

"Inborn genes, fixed for life, rolled when the dog is tamed and varying from one to the next. Unlike skills they never change with training; they are the raw nature this dog was born with."
Trait What it does
Strength Raises bite damage and, above all, saddlebag capacity
Aggressiveness Makes it earn Combat XP faster. It does not change whether it decides to fight or flee
Resistance Makes it build up less stress in a fight

There is also a fourth gene, for general stress, that never shows up in the window. It multiplies all the stress the dog picks up from fighting. The Caramelo has the worst one of all, the German Shepherd and the Husky the best.

Reading the labels

Label Means
Low Below 34% of the scale
Average Between 34% and 66%
High Above 66%

Careful: the labels sit on an absolute scale, not one relative to the breed. That is why a Caramelo shows Low on all three every single time, and why there is no such thing as an exceptional Caramelo. It is also why the Husky is pretty much the only dog you will ever see with High Strength.

How much this really matters

The genes move damage and combat XP by up to 15% either way, and stress resistance by up to 10 percentage points. The breed counts for far more than the gene.

The exception is the saddlebag: there the Strength gene is the main factor, and the difference between a weak dog and a strong one of the same breed is real.

There is no way to train a gene. The only way to improve them is to cross dogs.

9. Mixed breeds: crossing different breeds

When a male and a female of different breeds have puppies, you get a mixed breed.

What happens to the puppy

The lineage is the average of the parents. Caramelo with German Shepherd gives a 50/50 puppy. Crossing that puppy with a pure German Shepherd gives 25% Caramelo, 75% German Shepherd. And so on, generation after generation.

The looks are ONE of the breeds, drawn with the lineage as the weight. A 50/50 puppy is born looking like a German Shepherd or like a Caramelo, never like a visual blend. The more the lineage leans one way, the bigger the chance it comes out looking like that side.

And here is the part that confuses everyone: the breed that came up in the draw is the dog's functional breed. A 50% Husky / 50% German Shepherd mix born looking like a Husky is a Husky for every purpose: Warmed moodle, Husky learning speed, Husky saddlebag. The mixing does not put it halfway on any of that.

The genes land midway between the parents, give or take 6% either way.

And this is where mixing really pays off: the puppy's gene range becomes the union of the ranges of the breeds in its lineage. A Caramelo + Husky mix can be born with any Strength between 0.00 and 0.80, meaning it can break through the Caramelo's ceiling. By crossing the strongest dogs of each litter, you raise the whole lineage over the generations.

What the puppy does NOT inherit: any skill at all. It is born at level 0 in all five, always. What it inherits from the breed is the learning speed, not the learning.

The "Mixed" label

A dog with more than one breed in its lineage gets (Mixed) next to the breed name, in its window and in the context menu.

The percentage breakdown (50% Husky, 50% German Shepherd) shows up when you hover the Breed line in the dog's window, along with the description of the dominant breed.

Is crossing different breeds worth it?

Goal Answer
Improving genes over generations Yes, it is the only way there is
Getting the moodle of two breeds No. The dog has a single moodle, the one of the breed it became
Combining the learning speeds No. It uses the table of the breed it became
Making lots of puppies fast Use a Caramelo as the mother (2 to 4 per litter)

11. Breed FAQ

Which breed is the best? Depends on what you do. For combat, German Shepherd or Rottweiler. For hunting, Labrador. For cargo, Husky. For the farm, Border Collie. For firearms, Doberman. And the Caramelo is still excellent for its moodle, even being the weakest one.

Can my Caramelo get strong with training? No. It will never kill a zombie, no matter how much you train it, and its traits are the worst by definition. What training improves is Obedience, Scent and Hunting, and in those it goes just as far as any other dog.

I found two dogs of the same breed, which one do I take? You have no way of knowing beforehand: the roll happens at the moment of taming. Tame it and look at the Traits section in the window.

I got a dog with bad traits. Do I release it and tame it again? That won't help. The genes are rolled once only, on the first taming, and the dog keeps them forever, even after being released and tamed again (just like it keeps its skills). To try another roll you need to tame a different dog. To improve for real over time, the way is to cross them.

Can I have two breed moodles at the same time? No. Only the closest dog counts. Well Rested is the exception, because it doesn't depend on proximity.

Why did the moodle disappear? Almost always hunger or thirst above 75%, or loyalty having dropped below the floor. Feed it, give it water and pet it. Also check that the dog is 8 tiles or less away and on the same floor.

Is any breed faster? No. No breed runs faster than another.

Is there a Husky in another color? No. Each breed has a single texture, and males and females use the same one. The cat looks like it contradicts this with five coats, but those are five separate breeds, not one multicoloured cat.

Do big dogs hit harder? No. Size is purely visual. The link between body size and Strength was turned off on purpose.

Where do I find a Doberman? Police stations. The chance in a military building is higher on paper, but there is almost no military building on the game's base map.

I installed the Labrador and can't find any. Check your Companion Dogs base version: the Labrador requires 0.6.6 or newer and stays invisible and silent on an old base. Also check whether you have already explored the houses around there: the spawn decision is one per building, forever.

My mixed dog doesn't have the moodle of both breeds. That is expected. A mixed dog is, mechanically, the breed it looks like. The mixing only changes the gene range of its descendants and the percentage label in the window.

Is a cat better than a dog? Neither better nor worse: it does something else. It watches further than any dog and in silence, and it is the only companion that fixes night foraging. In exchange it does not fight, does not breed and carries nothing. A cat does not replace a guard dog; it fills a place no dog was filling.

Why can't I put my cat on Guard? Because the mode does not exist for it, which is why the button isn't there at all. A cat has no Combat: it does not guard, does not protect you and will not attack a zombie even while you are being hit beside it. This is not adjustable.

My cat ignores a rabbit standing right in front of it. That's correct. The cat's hunting ceiling is rodents: mice, rats and squirrels. Rabbits, raccoons and deer are invisible to it — it will not even point. For real hunting, the dog is a Labrador or a Golden.

How do I get kittens? You don't. Cats do not breed, with dogs or with other cats. Every cat in your game came from the world.

Do the five coats have different numbers? No, none at all. Same skills, same moodle, same trait ranges, same voice. The coat is appearance only, and which one you find is luck.

My cat went disobedient far too fast. That is the breed. A cat's bond fades 50% faster than a dog's when you disappear or slack off on care — the exact opposite of the Pug. A cat demands presence: petting, food and water, regularly.

I stopped waking up to gifts. Check its bars. A cat with hunger or thirst at 75% or more, sick, disobedient or panicking does not go out at night, and the failure is silent. Remember too that it is one per day, per owner: two cats do not give you two gifts.

I want to spawn a dog with another mod, which name do I use? The internal name, not the one shown on screen. The list is in Comparison tables, at the end of the section.
